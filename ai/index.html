Good questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need any experience with AI tools?

No. Every step tells you what to open, what to click and what to paste, and the Claude Co-Pilot fills in the prompts with you. If you can follow a recipe, you can follow this.

What tools do I need, and what do they cost?

Three: Higgsfield for images and video (paid credits), ElevenLabs for the voice (the Starter plan), and Claude for the co-pilot (Pro). CapCut is optional and free. The guide names every tool, setting and plan, and links you to Higgsfield.

Does Claude really do the hard parts?

Yes. The Claude Co-Pilot is included. Load it once and Claude writes your prompts, shapes your scripts, measures your audio and writes the second-by-second beat timeline for every video. You make the creative calls; it does the math.

Will my character look the same every time?

Yes. That's what the character sheet, the saved identity and the two references in every video prompt are for. The method shows you exactly how to lock the face so it holds across hundreds of images and videos.

Can I use myself, or a real person?

Yes, with written permission for anyone but you. There's a dedicated path for real people: their photos, a voice clone from one to three minutes of clean recording, and the fixes for when the likeness doesn't come out right.

How long until my first video?

Plan an afternoon for your first character and your first video. Every video after that is faster, because the character, voice and scene are already built and Claude does the timing.

What if the AI tools change?

They will. The system is written around the method, not one model's buttons, and updates are included. When the tools move, the guide moves with them.

Do I have to label my content as AI?

Yes, and the guide shows you how. Label it, get written permission for real people, and never present a character as a real customer. Done right, AI content performs and keeps your audience's trust.

How is this delivered?

Instantly, by email, as a digital download. You'll have it within a minute of checkout.

Can I get a refund?

Because it's a digital product with instant access, all sales are final. If something's broken, you can't find your download, or you get stuck, email us and we'll get you through it.
